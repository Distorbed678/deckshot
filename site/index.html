Every card stacks

Whoever loses picks an upgrade. Bouncing rockets, black holes, lightning on block, a turret on your shoulder.

Take the same card three times and find out what happens. The right pair unlocks a synergy you didn't know was there.

55 arenas that fight back

Saws, lasers, crushers, acid, ropes and breakable everything. Play Elimination or King of the Hill, free-for-all or 2v2, with a closing barrier that ends the stalling.

Bragging rights included

Every match ends with a recap: each player's numbers, final build and awards like Comeback Kid and Brick Wall. Save it as an image and send it to the friend you just beat.

Make your own

Build maps, cards and synergies in the built-in editors, then share them with a code. Vote on the next map, ban the card you hate, and earn about 60 cosmetics through 44 achievements.

Play with friends, however you can

Everyone in the same match, whatever they're playing on.

On your network

LAN play with no accounts and no internet needed.

On a phone
