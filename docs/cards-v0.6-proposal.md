# v0.6 card proposal (100 cards)

Nothing in `data/cards` has changed yet. Tell me which **current** cards to keep (list at the
bottom), and any changes to the cards below, and I'll swap them in and draw their art.

The design rules: one clear idea per card, most cards trade something away, every card stacks,
and cards combine through what they do. Names, numbers and art are Deckshot's own.
