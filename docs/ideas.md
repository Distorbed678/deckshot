# Ideas for later

Things we want but left out of v0.6.

## Game modes
- **Demolition:** each team has a physics tower with a core. Knock it down or break the core to win the round.
- **Rising Tide:** liquid climbs through the round while the map's physics pieces collapse, forcing everyone upward.
- **Crate Carry:** push, shoot or carry a physics crate into your zone to score.

## Features
- **Mutators:** round modifiers such as low gravity, everything has physics, or giant bullets.
- **Spectator camera:** a free camera for spectators.
- **Kill cams:** replay the last kill at the end of a round.
- **Accessibility:** colourblind palettes and a screen shake slider.
- **Weekly challenge:** a fixed map, mutator and card set that changes each week.
- **Players grabbing ropes:** press a key near a rope to hang on and swing.
